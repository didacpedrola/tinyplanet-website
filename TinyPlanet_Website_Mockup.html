Our story

We built the travel companion we wished existed.

Travel is one of the most powerful things you can do for a child's development. It builds empathy, curiosity, and a sense of wonder. But only if kids are actually present for it.

Tiny Planet gives kids a job to do, a story to tell, and a record to keep. It turns passive passengers into active explorers.

We design it to be the kind of thing kids pack themselves — not something parents have to convince them to use.
