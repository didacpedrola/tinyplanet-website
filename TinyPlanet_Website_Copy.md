## PAGE 4: HOW IT WORKS (standalone page, optional)

---

**Page Title:**
How Tiny Planet Works

**Intro:**
Tiny Planet isn't just a journal. It's a system — designed to make kids genuinely curious about the world they're traveling through.

---

**Before the trip**
Open the guide at home, days before you leave. Read about where you're going — the history, the animals, the food, the quirks. Set up your Explorer profile. Choose your mission goals. The excitement starts before you even pack.

**At the airport / on the way**
Use the travel challenges and transit games to make the journey part of the adventure. Spot things, answer questions, earn early stamps.

**Day by day**
Each day of the trip has space to journal, sketch, and record. Challenges push kids to notice things they'd otherwise miss — a building's architecture, a word in the local language, a food they've never tried.

**Back home**
The last section is for reflection — favourite moments, drawings, final stickers, and the all-important Explorer Rank reveal. Kids have a finished artefact they're proud of, and parents have a record of a trip well lived.
