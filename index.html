How it works

Your kid becomes the explorer.
You become the hero parent.

Three phases that turn any family holiday into an adventure they'll talk about for years.

Step 01
🗺️

Before the trip

Open the guide at home. Read about the history, culture, animals, and local food. Set up your Explorer profile and choose your mission goals. The excitement starts before you even pack.

Step 02
✏️

During the trip

Complete daily challenges, answer quizzes, spot local curiosities, and journal every discovery in real time. Every day is a new level to unlock.

Step 03
🌟

After the trip

Fill the final pages with photos, drawings, and memories. Earn stamps, complete the sticker collection, and discover your Explorer rank. The adventure lives on.

Our story

We built the travel companion we wished existed.

It started on a family trip. The kids had been looking forward to it for weeks — and then, somewhere between the flight and the first museum, the excitement faded. They weren't bored because they didn't care. They were bored because no one had given them a role in the story.

Travel is one of the most powerful things you can do for a child's development. It builds empathy, curiosity, and a sense of wonder. But only if kids are actually present for it.

Curiosity over consumption
We want kids asking questions, not just ticking sights off a list.
Stories over souvenirs
The best thing you can bring home is a memory.
